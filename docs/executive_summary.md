# Executive Summary

This project analyzes customer churn patterns in a European banking dataset using an interactive Streamlit dashboard. It helps identify which customer groups are most likely to leave, how churn varies by geography and behavior, and where the bank has the greatest balance exposure.

## Business Objectives

- Measure overall churn and retained customer base.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Compare churn behavior across countries, age groups, credit bands, tenure groups, and value tiers.
- Estimate balance exposure associated with churned customers.
- Recommend practical retention actions.

## Key Metrics

- Overall Churn Rate: Churned customers divided by total customers.
- Segment Churn Rate: Churned customers within a segment divided by total customers in that segment.
- Geographic Risk Index: Country churn rate divided by overall churn rate.
- Engagement Drop Indicator: Churn among inactive customers.
- Balance Exposure: Sum of balances held by churned customers.

## Recommended Actions

- Prioritize inactive customers with targeted reactivation campaigns.
- Review premium-tier churned and high-risk customers with relationship managers.
- Investigate countries with above-average geographic risk.
- Improve onboarding for newer customers.
- Monitor product ownership patterns, especially customers with unusually high churn by product count.
